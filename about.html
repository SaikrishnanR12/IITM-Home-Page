A little about me

I'm a quieter person by nature, and I've made peace with that — I'd rather think something through carefully than fill a room with noise. This portfolio is where that shows up: work I've sat with, questions I've followed through properly, and a record of what I'm slowly getting better at.

Right now that mostly means working through data — pulling real datasets apart, looking for the story inside the numbers, and being okay with not getting it right the first time.

Beyond the coursework

I spend a considerable amount of time looking up — not toward aerospace in the conventional engineering sense, but toward the deeper pull of space itself. I am drawn to the quiet mathematics of the cosmos, the strange elegance of astronomy, and science fiction that dares to imagine without abandoning the laws of physics. I follow the missions of ISRO, NASA, SpaceX, and others pushing the boundaries of what humanity can reach, one launch at a time. Perhaps it is curiosity. Perhaps it is the perspective that comes from contemplating something so vast that even our greatest achievements become remarkably small.

Then there is music — mostly lo-fi and instrumental, a quiet layer running beneath the architecture of everyday life. I sing too, though only for myself. Nothing rehearsed for an audience, nothing performed for recognition. It is simply another way of thinking — translating thoughts and emotions into something less precise, yet somehow more understood.
