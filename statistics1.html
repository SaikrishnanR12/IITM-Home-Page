COURSE OVERVIEW

Statistics 1

An introductory statistics course covering correlation, covariance, variance, distributions, and applied statistical reasoning on real-world datasets.

The course started with the fundamentals — sorting data into categorical and numerical types and figuring out which measurement scale each one sits on — before moving into ways to actually picture that data through bar, pie, and Pareto charts.
